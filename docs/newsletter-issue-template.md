## The question

Open with a question a reader might genuinely have about a map, place, dataset or decision. Keep the first paragraph understandable without specialist knowledge.

## The short answer

Explain the central idea in ordinary language. Give the reader a reason to keep going.

## See it

Use the clearest available map, diagram, dataset or worked example. Prefer a properly licensed source figure when it explains the idea better than a new drawing; otherwise link to the original or make an original visual. State what the reader should notice and credit the source directly below the figure.

## What is happening underneath?

Move from intuition into the actual method:
- the problem and inputs;
- the mathematical or computational idea;
- the main steps;
- assumptions and limits;
- where implementation choices matter.

Define technical terms when they first become necessary. Do not hide important complexity; introduce it at the point where it helps explain the result.

## Try it yourself

Give the reader a short exercise or reproducible experiment. Link to the canonical algorithm record and tested code where available. Let readers predict first, run the example, change an input and compare what happened.

## Where this is useful

Connect the method to real applications. Be specific about what it can and cannot tell someone.

## Keep exploring

Point to one related method, one learning path and one useful source. Avoid a generic link dump.

## Editorial checks

- Can a new reader understand why this topic matters from the opening?
- Is the short answer accurate without relying on unexplained jargon?
- Does the example genuinely demonstrate the method?
- Are claims separated into mathematical facts, implementation behaviour and experiment results?
- Are versions, units, CRS and precision choices recorded where relevant?
- Does the public edition provide real value on its own?
- Does the member edition add meaningful depth rather than withholding the basic explanation?
- Do equations render as mathematics, with every symbol explained?
- Are diagrams legible, accessible and correctly attributed?
- Are all links and references checked?
