## Editorial cautions preserved from the source

The compendium itself flags several later sections as memory-based or representative and says names should be verified before citation. Explicit warnings occur in sections 11, 13, 15, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22. Section 12 also describes its methods as additions from adjacent fields. These flags are not reasons to discard entries; they are reasons to prioritise verification before treating them as established claims.

## Important taxonomy observation

The source is intentionally broad. It includes algorithms, operations, statistical models, data structures, metrics, software systems, standards, sensor-processing workflows, applied methods and named datasets/products. The catalogue should retain a `kind` field so that the project does not mislabel every item as an algorithm.
