# Research notes

This directory is for focused investigations that support the canonical explanations. It is not a dumping ground for links or unreviewed claims.

## What belongs here

- literature reviews and source comparisons;
- notes on competing definitions or algorithm variants;
- verification of names and claims from the original compendium;
- implementation behaviour that needs investigation;
- unresolved questions and proposed experiments;
- notes that are useful before a canonical record is ready.

## Keep the evidence visible

A research note should record:

- the question being investigated;
- the sources consulted, with stable links and publication/version details;
- what each source actually supports;
- disagreements, assumptions and missing evidence;
- what was verified directly;
- what remains uncertain;
- the decision or next step for the canonical record.

Distinguish a claim from an interpretation. Do not treat a search result, abstract or remembered method name as verification of details that require the full source.

## From research note to published explanation

When the work is ready, update the relevant canonical record under [algorithms](../algorithms/README.md), implementation, exercise or learning path. Link back to the research note when the reasoning or unresolved questions are useful to readers.

The source compendium's page transcriptions remain preserved under [source inventory](../source-inventory/README.md). They are intake material, not independent verification.
