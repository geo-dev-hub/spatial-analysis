# Exercise: maximal covering location

## Tasks

1. Solve MCLP for p=1, p=2 and p=3 using a 10-minute service threshold.
2. Repeat at 15 minutes. Which demand locations change status?
3. Compare the results with p-median on the same cost matrix.
4. Construct an asymmetric cost matrix and explain how one-way travel changes the result.
5. Identify a case where the at-most-p model opens fewer than p facilities.
6. Propose a capacity constraint. Explain why the current model cannot represent queues or limited service capacity.
7. Write a short policy note explaining why the service threshold is justified.

Record cost units, threshold, demand weights, selected candidates, covered population, uncovered locations, tie-breaking rule and limitations. Never report a covered share without its denominator.
