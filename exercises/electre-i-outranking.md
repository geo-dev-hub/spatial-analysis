# Exercise: inspect an outranking relation

Use the [ELECTRE I-style Python package](../implementations/python/electre-i/README.md) to compare three candidate sites.

1. Define at least three criteria, including a benefit and a cost.
2. Record a clear reason for each criterion weight.
3. Calculate the concordance and discordance matrices.
4. Identify pairs that outrank one another and pairs that remain incomparable.
5. Change the concordance and discordance thresholds and report which pairwise relations change.
6. Explain why the result is not automatically a complete ranking or a final site-selection decision.

Read the [ELECTRE method-family overview](../methods/electre-outranking.md) before interpreting the result.
