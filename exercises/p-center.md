# Exercise: discrete p-center

1. Solve the visual-lab example for p=1, 2 and 3.
2. Identify the demand location determining the maximum assignment cost.
3. Add a remote demand point and explain the change in the optimal radius.
4. Compare p-center with p-median and maximal covering on the same cost matrix.
5. Replace symmetric costs with directed travel times and inspect the effect.
6. State the assumptions required before interpreting the result as a service guarantee.

Record the demand set, candidate set, cost units, routing assumptions, selected sites, assignments, worst-served location and model limitations.
